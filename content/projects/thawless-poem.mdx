I had never thought of writing poems until my English teacher told me to for a project. We all submitted our poems, and mine happened to make it through the selection phase.

The Polar Expressions Writing Contest then told me that my poem, "Thawless", would be featured in their publication, *The Shape of Words*. I reported this in my March 2026 newsletter.

Writing has always been fun for me, because it lets me express myself.
