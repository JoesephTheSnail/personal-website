I started this page in the spring of 2025 because I wanted to track my progress.

I posted a few kinds of videos:
- My exercises and workouts.
- The meals I eat.
- How I recover properly.
- Food hacks.
- Sometimes a day in my life.

One video reached a peak of 350K views. I don't remember my other numbers. I later stopped posting and deleted the app from my phone to focus on school this year.

I filmed everything myself and edited it in the TikTok app.

**What I took from it:**

Filming in the gym felt scary at first. Over time I stopped worrying about it. I also learned how to make videos that hook viewers, and I got over my fear of talking to a camera and caring what other people think.

I mostly wanted to share my journey, and hopefully learn from the mistakes I made along the way.
