Through DECA, my team and I joined a campaign to raise awareness about pediatric cancer.

Cancer is the second leading cause of death in Canada. I have a lot of respect for the people who go through it, and it's worse when it happens to someone young. Many people don't know how many kids suffer from it.

One way we spread the word was a booth at a school open house. We talked with a lot of parents, and it was lovely to see how much they cared.
