The idea was to make procrastination visible: not one dramatic failure, but the quiet, repeated choice to put things off that reshapes a life without ever feeling like a single decision.

## How it was made

Shot entirely on an iPhone, with no crew or rented gear. That meant:

- Learning colour grading to keep a consistent mood across scenes shot at different times of day
- Building the sound design from scratch
- Working out framing and lighting with whatever was on hand

**What I took from it:**
- Most "amateur" footage comes down to untouched audio or bad light, not gear. Both are fixable with attention, not budget.
- Making a film about procrastination while fighting the urge to procrastinate on making it was its own lesson.
