I was scrolling one night and got bored of it. So I learned something I'd always thought was a cool party trick: working out what day of the week any date falls on, all in your head.

## How it works

Add up four small numbers: one for the year, one for the month, one for the day, and one for the century. Divide by 7. The remainder is the day.

Once you know the keys, it takes a few seconds per date.

## The project

I made a one-page reference that explains it, and worked through the math myself until it made sense instead of just being memorized.

It's a small project, but a good reminder: the time I'd have spent doomscrolling can just as easily go toward learning something random and fun.
