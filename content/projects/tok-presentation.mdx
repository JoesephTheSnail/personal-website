Theory of Knowledge combines epistemology and self-reflection. It asks what we know and why we bother knowing it at all.

## The question

Why do humans seek knowledge?

The obvious answer is utility: knowing things helps us survive, build, and succeed. But that doesn't explain why people read books they'll never use, research questions with no practical stakes, or feel real satisfaction from understanding something abstract.

## The other answer

Curiosity is a drive in itself. There's something about making sense of a thing that was unclear that people pursue whatever the outcome. The question is whether that drive is built into human thinking or produced by culture.

## The argument

The presentation argues that both are true, and that the tension between them is where most of the interesting intellectual work happens.
