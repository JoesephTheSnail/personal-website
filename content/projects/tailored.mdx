AnyWear taught me a lot. Tailored is what happens when you take those lessons seriously.

## What's the same

It's still peer-to-peer fashion rental that makes designer clothing accessible without ownership.

## What's different

- **A tighter business model** and clearer unit economics.
- **A working app demo,** which AnyWear didn't have.
- **Objections already answered.** After three pitch rounds at DragonzDen, I knew where the holes were: trust between strangers, insurance for damaged items, and the cold-start problem of building a two-sided marketplace from zero.
- **A new framing.** AnyWear was about sustainability and savings. Tailored is about access: the clothes you wear to a once-in-a-while event don't need to be yours.

The target user cares about looking great but doesn't want to pay retail for an outfit they'll wear once.

This project is ongoing.
