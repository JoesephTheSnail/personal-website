This year my school began livestreaming its announcements, so parents and students know what's happening each day. It sounds simple, but because it's live, the five minutes before we go on air are the most stressful.

## My role

I'm in charge of making sure the assembly runs smoothly, and I love it. I already love film and the technology behind it, and this puts the two together.

I'm only one person, so I had to learn to delegate. Organizing a group is hard, but I know it's a big skill to build if I want to do anything in business.

![A black teleprompter on a table, with its glass held at an angle inside a fabric hood.](/projects/school-livestream-teleprompter.jpg)

*Setting up our teleprompter.*

## The funny moments

It's new for both the school and me. Once we forgot to mute our mics while setting up, and everyone heard us panicking. Another time we cut to the outro, and a friend didn't realize the mics were still on and did a funny celebration.

This is where it stood in my November 2026 newsletter. I hoped to get better at each assembly over the next six months.
