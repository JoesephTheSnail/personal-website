AnyWear is a peer-to-peer luxury clothing rental platform. The idea is that you shouldn't have to own something to wear it once.

## Who it's for

People who want to look great at a formal event, a photoshoot, or a night out without paying retail for an outfit they'll wear once.

## How it works

- **Owners** list high-end items for rent and earn passive income from clothes sitting in their closet.
- **Renters** get designer pieces for a fraction of the purchase price.

## The pitches

We took AnyWear through three full pitch rounds at DragonzDen. Each round, the judges pushed harder on margins, churn, insurance liability, and the cold-start problem of building a two-sided marketplace from zero.

I wrote about the March round in my newsletter while we were still in the middle of it, dealing with imposter syndrome and unsure we'd make it through. The feedback was hard. We took it seriously and came back sharper each time.

**Result:** 1st place at DragonzDen.

When I wrote about winning in my May 2025 newsletter, I kept coming back to the process, not the trophy: three pitches, and three rounds of judges who didn't owe us anything. The version that won was very different from the one we started with. That difference came entirely from pressure.

The biggest takeaway: a pitch is only as strong as the business model under it. We won because we knew our numbers cold and had thought through the edge cases before anyone asked.
