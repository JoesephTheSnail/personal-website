Everyone says no screens before bed, but nobody says for how long. So I ran my own study: 8 people, 85 nights, split into three groups:

- No restriction
- 30 minutes screen-free
- 60 minutes screen-free

## How it went

Collecting the data took about 20 days. The rest of the 8 months went into writing it up and getting the stats right. With only 8 people, you can't just eyeball the results.

## What I found

- **30 minutes screen-free** made people feel noticeably more rested.
- **60 minutes** didn't add anything on top of that.
- **Wake-ups overnight** didn't really change either way. So it looks more like a wind-down effect than a real change in sleep quality.

**Outcome:** An 8-person, 85-night study finding that 30 minutes screen-free before bed improves how rested people feel, with no added benefit from a full hour.

**What I took from it:**
- "No screens before bed" is basically right, but nobody tests how long. That's where this got interesting.
- Chasing 8 people to log data every night for 20 days was harder than any of the analysis.
- Small samples are messy. Most of the 8 months went into picking statistics that wouldn't lie to me about 8 people's data.
