- Changing someone's mind is usually less about pushing harder and more about removing whatever's holding them back. Reduce the friction before adding more force.
- People resist being told what to do (reactance) far more than they resist the idea itself. Letting someone arrive at a conclusion on their own sticks better than arguing them into it.
- Uncertainty kills action even when the upside is obvious. Making something easy to try, not just easy to want, is what actually moves people.
