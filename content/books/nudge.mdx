- Most behaviour is driven by cognitive shortcuts rather than deliberate reasoning. We are the product of our defaults.
- The brain operates in two modes: *automatic* (intuitive, fast) and *reflective* (deliberate, slow). Automatic thinking tends to lead us astray on complex decisions, which is why we cave to cravings we know we'll regret.
